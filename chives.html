Chives

Allium schoenoprasum

Fresh, grassy, onion-flavoured leaves for everyday cooking — and purple flowers that pollinators love.

Explore how chives grow →

What are chives?

Chives are perennial herbs in the onion family. Their thin, hollow green leaves add a gentle onion flavour to salads, soups, eggs, and potatoes.

Botanical facts

Scientific name
Allium schoenoprasum
Plant type
Perennial
Edible parts
Leaves and flowers

Growing chives

☀ Light

Choose a sunny position. Chives can also grow in partial shade.

💧 Water

Keep soil evenly moist without letting the pot stay waterlogged.

✂ Harvest

Snip leaves regularly, leaving a short base so they can regrow.

Using chives in the kitchen

Add freshly chopped chives near the end of cooking to retain their delicate taste. They work especially well with potatoes, eggs, soups and dips.

Try chives with plain yogurt, a little lemon juice and black pepper for a quick herb dip.
